# Public action-conditional native signal diagnostic v1

`advisor.learned_action_signals` is an opt-in coordinator diagnostic, not a new
policy, reward, exact action-value authority, or production ranking consumer.
Original categorical V4 features, weights, PPO and historical models are intact.

## Boundary

Construct complete **explicit hypothetical** six-member teams and ordered
bring-three choices, then use native TeamValidator/reset and native legal prefix
execution. Admit hypotheses only if every delivered player-view field agrees,
including public history, own request, projected state and 35-action choices and
masks. Only episode ID, revision and incremental `events` transport are ignored;
the accumulated history is retained. This is stricter than encoder equality.
Unknown opponent information stays unknown in the policy view. Hypothesis sets,
their weights and assumed bench/preview choices are assumptions, not observations
or an inferred posterior. A replay-consistent prefix is a bounded witness, not a
general belief-state reconstruction algorithm.

Checkpoints stay coordinator-owned. `ShowdownEnvironment.restart(checkpoint,
future_seed=[a,b,c,d])` optionally replaces only the maintained native PRNG using
the existing `bridge.cjs` sampling pattern. It leaves the saved checkpoint and
public decision unchanged and records future-seed provenance in the new branch.
Omitting the argument retains original restore semantics. This is hypothetical
future sampling, **not** exact restoration of the historical random trajectory.
Equal initial future seeds do not guarantee identical random events after action
or policy divergence. Seeds, hypotheses and private opposing requests never enter
the actor or critic. Each player receives only its own native public observation.

## Evidence

* Compare only native request-mask-legal candidate actions at an exact public
  decision. Coverage-based representatives are not an exhaustive search.
* A request-legal action can still be natively rejected on previously hidden
  trapping information. Record the rejection and updated public request; never
  assign a terminal label to a rejected first action or silently substitute one.
* Record the first request-boundary public consequences separately from an
  eight-boundary continuation position/value estimate and eventual native result.
  A boundary can be a pivot/replacement request, not a complete turn.
* An artificial timeout has no completed-game return label. Native terminal
  win/loss/draw returns are +1/-1/0 for the candidate's actual seat.
* All returns are conditional on hypothetical sets and fixed public continuation
  policies. They are not optimal-action labels, exact probabilities, or a causal
  estimate of actual strategic strength.

`scripts/evaluate_showdown_action_signals.py --plan <frozen.json> --output <external-directory>`
runs a bounded Windows CPU job with existing resource/STOP protections. The plan
binds model hashes, hypotheses, native seeds and the discovery/confirmation gate.
Complete per-state records can be resumed under the same plan hash. A partially
executed unsaved state is not claimed as exactly recovered. Keep all raw records,
private hypothesis manifests and selected public roots outside Git.

Before optional auxiliary learning, split future samples into discovery and
confirmation, test conditional action-return differences, hypothesis/rival sign
changes, probability on inferior candidates, missing labels and effective case
coverage. Small fixed-hypothesis bootstrap intervals cannot establish robustness
to unmodeled sets, new states or training seeds. If this gate fails, do not force
an auxiliary PPO experiment or replace native rewards with these scores.

## Bounded v1 result (2026-10-15)

The frozen corrected panel contained six motifs in both actual native seats,
three public-equivalent hypotheses per root, five actions, two fixed public
rivals and eight future seeds: 2,880 completed terminal branches, 77,592 native
decision boundaries. No truncation or native choice rejection occurred. Complete
four-move V8 training-bank teammates were used; an earlier single-move legality
witness bench preflight was stopped and excluded rather than interpreted as a
competitive learning signal.

The first four future seeds selected a comparison; the other four confirmed it.
Only the injured-Slowbro recovery-versus-attack motif passed the preregistered
conditional gap/robustness/policy-exposure checks (both seats, one motif). The
required four roots across at least two motifs did not pass. Across 120 candidate
pairs, 38 reversed mean sign between hypotheses, eight between fixed rivals and
68 between individual future-seed blocks (overlapping categories, descriptive
counts). There were useful local signals, but no sufficiently broad, robust
auxiliary dataset. **No auxiliary optimization or new PPO training was run.**

Intervals describe four confirmation seed blocks conditional on the chosen
hypotheses/rivals; they do not represent hidden-set prior uncertainty, independent
training seeds, or generalization. The two seat outcomes were identical in this
panel, so twelve roots are not twelve independent strategic situations. V8 team
eligibility/complete movesets do not establish competitive team-distribution
realism. No model strength or optimal-action conclusion follows from this test.
