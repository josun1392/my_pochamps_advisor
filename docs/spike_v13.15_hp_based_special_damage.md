# v13.15 HP-Based Special Damage

Endeavor and Final Gambit use only exact confirmed self/opponent HP under
`explicit-hp-based-special-damage-only`. Endeavor lowers only a higher target
to the user's current HP. Final Gambit deals user current HP capped by target
HP and records a self-faint consequence, not recoil. Normal/fixed formulas and
post-faint mechanics are excluded.
