# v15.84 defender-ability provider grounding smoke

## Fixture contract

The approval-gated fixture pair retains the minimal multi-provider response:
resolved status, deterministic rank-one candidate ID, and a bounded
explanation code. `supported-defender-ability-candidates` uses a
user-confirmed opponent Fur Coat. A physical fixed-hit candidate receives the
server-owned reduction tag, while a special formula candidate does not and a
level-based fixed-damage candidate remains outside ordinary defender
modifiers. The fixture fixes the trusted level at one so the supported
fixed-hit candidate is the deterministic rank-one result.

`unsupported-defender-ability-with-level-fixed-control` uses known Solid
Rock. Its formula candidate remains unsupported; Seismic Toss is the usable
level-based control candidate and has no ordinary defender-ability tag.

## Authority variants and presentation

Unknown and malformed target ability identities remain provider-free
insufficient or unsupported states. A fresh request without an opponent
ability context does not reuse a prior target ability. A response attempting a
non-rank-one candidate is rejected before result binding. Presentation checks
use only the selected candidate's Korean Fur Coat label and reject internal
tags or unselected evidence.

## Actual boundary

The actual runner may invoke only these two fixtures with `gemini-2.5-flash`,
two calls in a round, and no retry, fallback, or repair. Provider output stays
limited to selection and explanation code; target ability, conditions,
modifiers, damage, percent, KO, and Q12 rounding remain server-owned.
