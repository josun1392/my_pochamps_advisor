# Predictive attack authority v1

The first present-tense predictive attack contract is
`deterministic-predictive-attack-authority-v1`. It is distinct from an
observed result: it is D0-bound, pure, and never records that an attack has
already occurred.

The representative is Seismic Toss. Its damage is exactly the known attacker
level, so it avoids normal-damage random rolls. Exact prediction needs D0-bound
attacker/target identities, target current HP, attacker level, target current
types, and known Substitute state. Item, ability, weather, and combat stats are
not inputs for this fixed-damage slice. Unknown material authority remains
incomplete; it is never assumed neutral.

Formula moves retain their full legal damage-roll range. They cannot be
converted into one exact outcome without a future interval or predictive branch
set contract. The current candidate materializer expects one exact outcome
branch and still accepts only observed damage authority; it remains unchanged.
A later predictive-outcome adapter must translate exact-complete predictive
authority into a detached branch. Ranking may only consume guaranteed terminal
facts or exact HP from that future adapter, never a guessed roll.
