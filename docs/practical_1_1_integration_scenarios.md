# Practical 1.1 deterministic integration scenarios

The sanitized `test_v60_practical_1_1_integration_scenarios.py` suite crosses
the supported lifecycle, reducer, runtime projection, frozen snapshot, and
native direct-Q12 seams. It covers representative—not exhaustive—Practical
1.1 paths:

- Rain Dish, Ice Body, Solar Power, and Dry Skin at confirmed first EOT;
- Black Sludge current-type ownership and switch/snapshot isolation;
- toxic progression, Life Orb's trusted qualifying-hit recoil, and lethal
  Sandstorm residual state;
- type-resist berries including Chilan plus Flail/Reversal direct-Q12 power.

The scenarios also retain conservative authority behavior: observations remain
identity-bound, frozen state stays detached, and no unobserved event, weather,
ability, type, item, or EOT ordering is inferred. Broader pairwise EOT ordering
and general trigger simulation remain outside Practical 1.1.
